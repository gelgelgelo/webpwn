Welcome to Webpwn

A place to develop and hack at the same time

Our Goal

The goal is to develop critical website functionalities that's intentionaly
vulnerable so we can exploit it and fix it and learn from it.

Develop what?

  • Authentication (login and signup)
  • Profile customization (edit profile picture, bio, and etc.)
  • Interaction with other users (threads; posts, comments, and replies)
  • Administrative account or higher privilege user.

Exploit what?

  • Auth Bypass and other Auth vulnerability tricks
  • IDOR/Broken Access Control vulns, file uploads, XSS, and etc.
  • Information Disclosure and other cool stuff.
  • Privelege Escalation or other ways we can access the higher priv user.
